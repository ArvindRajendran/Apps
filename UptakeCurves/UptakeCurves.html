Uptake Curves — Diffusion in Bidisperse Adsorbents

Predicts a gravimetric uptake experiment: a step change of sorbate fraction y0→y1 in an inert carrier at constant total pressure, single component, isothermal spheres. Choose micropore (crystal) diffusion, macropore diffusion, the full bidisperse (macro + micro) model, or an LDF lumped model. Darken’s thermodynamic correction available for micropore diffusion. Equilibrium at the outer surface; symmetry at both centres.
