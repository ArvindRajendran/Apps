System: (I + ν ∂q/∂c) ∂c/∂τ + ∂c/∂ξ = 0, ν = (1−ε)/ε; two characteristic families σk = 1/(1+νλk). Constant-selectivity Langmuir: exact straight-line hodograph (h-transformation, Rhee–Aris–Amundson); wave and shock images coincide.
General isotherms: conservative 2nd-order MUSCL reference solution in the self-similar variable s = ξ/τ (numerically generated hodograph). Works offline — single file, share freely.
