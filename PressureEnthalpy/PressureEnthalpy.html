Pressure–Enthalpy Diagram

Real-fluid P–h chart (NIST-quality embedded property data): hover for state values, click to place points A–D, toggle property lines.

State points

Click the chart to place A, B, C, D in turn; drag to move. Or type P + T (single phase) or P + x (two-phase) and press Enter. The process path chains the placed points in order.

Cycle builder

Computes the cycle and places its states as points A–D (A = compressor/turbine inlet). Manually moving or editing a point switches the cycle off.
Property data precomputed with CoolProp (reference EOS: Span–Wagner 1996 for CO₂, IAPWS-95 for water, Bücker–Wagner 2006 for ethane); h/s reference states follow each EOS's published (NIST WebBook default) convention.
