IAST — Binary Ideal Adsorbed Solution Theory

Mixture adsorption equilibria predicted from two pure-component isotherms (Myers & Prausnitz 1965): equal spreading pressures ψ1(p₁⁰) = ψ2(p₂⁰) with Pyi = xipi0. Spreading pressures are analytic for most models; the solver bisects a strictly monotone function of x1, so the solution is unique and always found. IAST: ψi(p) = ∫₀p qi0/p′ dp′, equal ψ at the hypothetical pressures pi0 = Pyi/xi, and 1/qT = Σ xi/qi0(pi0). Selectivity obeys S = p₂⁰/p₁⁰, a function of ψ alone — hence binary IAST admits no composition azeotropes: the x–y curve can only touch the diagonal at the pressure p* where ψ₁ and ψ₂ cross (selectivity reversal), marked when present. Watch the pi0 readouts: the weaker component is evaluated far above P (unbounded extrapolation of its pure isotherm). Validated against exact limits and cross-validated against pyIAST (see README).
